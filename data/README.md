# Dataset files

Download the TMDB 5000 Movie Metadata CSV files from https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/tmdb/tmdb-movie-metadata and upload them in the app:

- `tmdb_5000_movies.csv`
- `tmdb_5000_credits.csv`

The app does not bundle the dataset. Review its terms and attribution requirements before use. Keep downloaded CSV files out of Git; `.gitignore` excludes them.
